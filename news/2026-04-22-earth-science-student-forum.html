Earth Science Student Forum - Buffalo Museum of Science

Harrison Van Lone presented his research at the 2026 Earth Science Student Forum, hosted by the UB Department of Earth Sciences and the Buffalo Museum of Science. The forum brought together undergraduate earth/environmental science students from universities across Western New York, with students sharing their research through poster presentations.

Harrison presented his work on modeling stream temperature using the River Basin Model (RBM-2L).
