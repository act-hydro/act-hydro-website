Welcome Sonam!

We are thrilled to welcome Sonam to the a.c.t. hydro lab this fall!

Sonam joins the lab as an M.S. student in Computational Earth Science. Originally from Nepal, he holds a B.E. in Civil Engineering from Pulchowk Campus, Tribhuvan University, and worked as an engineer with Nepal's Department of Water Resources and Irrigation. As part of our NSF DISES project, his research examines how reservoir operations and climate change affect streamflow and river temperatures in Mountain West rivers. For more information, please check his page.
