CAS Undergraduate Research Showcase 2026

A.C.T. Hydro undergraduate researchers present their work!

Undergraduate researchers Harrison Van Lone and Dominic Tubiolo presented at the 2026 College of Arts and Sciences Undergraduate Research Showcase at the University at Buffalo.

Their poster, "Modeling Stream Temperature in Response to Climate and Hydrologic Change in Mountain West River Basins," presented the use of river temperature modeling to evaluate stream temperature conditions across the Mountain West, with a focus on conditions relevant to cold-water fish such as trout.
